| Outcome | Tool result |
| --- | --- |
| Approve and launch reports `queued`, `accepted`, or `confirmed` | Success with `note_path`, `write_status`, the reported `launch_status`, `operation_id`, and `session_key`. |
| Write fails or cannot be confirmed | `E_TOOL_FAILED` with `note_path`, `write_status: failed` or `unknown`, and `launch_status: not_started`. |
| Launch fails, is cancelled by the launcher, or has an unknown outcome after a confirmed write | `E_TOOL_FAILED` with the written `note_path`, launch status, and operation or session identifiers when available. |
| Stop after a confirmed write and before launch | `E_TURN_ABORTED` with the written `note_path` and `launch_status: not_started`. |
| Reject, expiry, or stop while waiting | `E_APPROVAL_DENIED`, `E_APPROVAL_EXPIRED`, or `E_TURN_ABORTED`, respectively. Each runs zero MCP calls. |
| Valid Change submission | `{ changes_requested: "<user text>" }`. The card shows "Changes requested," and any revised proposal needs fresh approval. |
| Stale, late, or unauthorized Change submission | The modal shows why the submission was refused. The submission runs zero MCP calls. |
| Slack fails to post the card | `E_SLACK`. The request runs zero MCP calls. |
